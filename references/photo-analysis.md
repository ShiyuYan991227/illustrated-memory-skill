# Photo Analysis

Analyze only what is directly visible in the photo.

Return concise notes for:

1. Main subject or subjects
2. Environment
3. Composition and spatial relationships
4. Lighting direction and distinctive light or shadow details
5. Dominant colors
6. One small moment worth remembering
7. Overall emotional tone

Do not infer identity, location, hidden context, weather, or time of day unless it is visually clear.
