# CAPTION_PROMPT — Illustrated Memory

Look at the source photograph and write one short English caption for a quiet illustrated journal page.

Describe one small, specific moment that is visibly supported by the image rather than summarizing the whole photograph.

Tone: observational, gentle, slightly poetic, natural, and effortless — as if someone casually wrote down a moment they wanted to remember.

Focus on concrete sensory details such as sunlight, shadows, breeze, leaves, movement, weather, small animals, streets, windows, water, food, or everyday objects.

Rules:
- 7–16 words in most cases
- one sentence or one natural phrase
- simple everyday English
- no motivational language
- no abstract life philosophy
- no generic inspirational quote
- no exaggerated sentimentality
- no overly literary vocabulary
- do not start with “This photo”, “A beautiful”, “Life is”, “Sometimes”, or “Remember”

Good examples:
- Under the dappled light, a little cat quietly passes by.
- The afternoon sun settles softly between the leaves.
- A small breeze finds its way through the summer trees.
- Sunlight drifts across the wall as the courtyard stays quiet.

Output only the caption.
