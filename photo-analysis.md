# PHOTO_ANALYSIS_PROMPT

Analyze only what is directly visible in the photo. Return concise notes for:
1. Main subject(s)
2. Environment
3. Composition and spatial relationships
4. Lighting direction and distinctive light/shadow details
5. Dominant colors
6. One small moment worth remembering
7. Overall emotional tone

Do not infer identity, location, hidden context, weather, or time of day unless visually clear.
